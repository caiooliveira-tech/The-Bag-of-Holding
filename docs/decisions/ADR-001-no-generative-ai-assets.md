# ADR-001 - No Generative AI for Art or Audio Assets

## Context

Building for GMTK Jam 2026 under a ~4-day deadline. Generative AI image and audio tools could speed up asset production under time pressure. Some distribution platforms (e.g. Steam) require disclosure/labeling for AI-generated content, which can affect a game's visibility and reception.

## Decision

All art and audio assets will be created manually by the team (Design/Visual front: Silas Chosen, Flavio Lee) or sourced from free, non-AI-generated external libraries. No generative AI tooling will be used for art or audio assets.

This does not restrict AI-assisted coding tools for programming tasks — the project's own CLAUDE.md working agreement already relies on an AI coding assistant for implementation, and that remains unchanged.

## Alternatives Considered

Using generative AI (image/audio) to accelerate asset production under jam time pressure — rejected.

## Consequences

- Slower asset production under an already tight deadline; the team must budget explicit hours for manual art and audio sourcing.
- Reduces risk of AI-disclosure penalties or reputational concerns on distribution platforms.
- Preserves authenticity of the human-made creative work as a stated project value.
