# Gameplay Pillars

## 1. Countdown Creates Pressure

The countdown begins immediately after drawing an item.

Every decision happens under time pressure.

---

## 2. Risk vs Reward

Holding an item increases tactical opportunities.

Holding it too long becomes dangerous.

---

## 3. Improvisation

Players never know what item comes next.

Adaptation is more valuable than planning.

---

## 4. Friendly Fire

The player's own items are always dangerous.

Good positioning is mandatory.

---

## 5. Readability

Players should understand:

- danger
- countdown
- damage
- state changes

without reading numbers.

---

## 6. Fast Combat

Combat should stay short.

Rooms should feel dynamic.

Movement should rarely stop.

---

## 7. Graybox First

Gameplay is validated before visual polish.

Primitive shapes are acceptable until mechanics are proven.

---

## 8. Playability Over Completeness

A playable build is more valuable than ten unfinished systems.

Every feature should improve the next playable version.

---

## 9. One Core Mechanic

The Bag of Holding is the game's identity.

Every new feature should reinforce this mechanic.

If a feature distracts from the Bag, reconsider adding it.
